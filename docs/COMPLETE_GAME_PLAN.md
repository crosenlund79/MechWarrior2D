### Campaign length estimate
Critical path: about 12 map transits plus 3 story hunts and the finale. With the economy pacing (a
territory hunt-and-return takes 10–25 minutes; the gear the late regions need costs 5–15 k Mark), a
first playthrough is estimated at **6–10 hours**, below the brief's 10–15 h target. This is an
estimate from the economy numbers, not a measured playtest.

### Remaining work (next iterations)
1. Day/night cycle with headlights and night behaviour for the skirr families.

3. Field guide page with the species tallies and apex records; personal hunting records.
4. More authored contracts and side stories per region (toward the 10–15 h target); more distinct
   settlement layouts; hand-placed set pieces per territory.
5. Creator art passes: portraits, new species, Stahlheim, chassis.
6. Balance passes from real playtests (apex health, prices, fuel), Windows playtest of the build.
